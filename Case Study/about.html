About PowerConnect

PowerConnect is an electricity outage information and reporting service designed to help residents and businesses report electricity problems quickly and safely.

The system provides outage checking, safety information, incident reporting, restoration estimates and status updates.

Our objective is to provide clear and trustworthy information while helping electricity service teams respond efficiently.

Fast Reporting

Report electricity outages using only essential information.

Safety Focused

Important electrical safety information is shown before reporting.

Clear Information

Users can distinguish between reports, verified outages and restoration estimates.

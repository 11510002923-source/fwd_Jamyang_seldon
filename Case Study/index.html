SAFETY INFORMATION

Electrical Safety First

If you see sparks, smoke, damaged poles, fallen wires or exposed electrical equipment, stay away from the area.

Never approach damaged electrical equipment.

Stay at a safe distance and report the hazard.

Do
  • Stay at least 10 metres from fallen lines
  • Keep children and pets away from the area
  • Switch off appliances until power returns
  • Use a torch instead of candles
Don't
  • Touch wires, poles or anything near them
  • Drive over or under fallen lines
  • Try to repair the fault yourself
  • Run a generator indoors
